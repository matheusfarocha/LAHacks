RoadGuard G2 prototype

Experimental G2 head-nod pattern monitoring with parked-only calibration, GPS speed, and rest reminders. No server, additional app, external API, or account.

Connecting...

Experimental, not an alertness detector. Repeated head-nod-like movements may trigger a steady on-glasses warning, but ordinary head motions can also trigger it and sleepiness may go undetected. There is deliberately no strobe. Only calibrate or acknowledge alerts when parked. If sleepy, stop safely and rest.
